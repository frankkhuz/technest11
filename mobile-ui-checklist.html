The 28 items

  2. 2Native app-like experienceDone

    Fixed top and bottom bars, bottom-sheet filters, no website footer on phones, notch spacing. The site can now be installed to the home screen with its own icon, opens full-screen, and shows a proper "You're offline" screen with no connection.

    No swipe-back gestures (the phone's own back gesture still works).

  3. 3Fixed top navigation barDone

    The top bar is fixed to the screen. Checked: it stays at the top while scrolling on every page.

  4. 4Fixed bottom navigation bar (Home, Categories, Flash Sales, Cart, Account)Done

    Exactly these five items. Flash Sales is a raised glossy centre button and Cart shows the item count.

    "Sell" and "Fix" moved from the bottom bar to the ☰ menu.

  5. 5Prevent horizontal scrollingDone

    The page can no longer move sideways. Checked on 29 pages (shop, account, orders, requests, disputes, dashboards, admin) at 320–414px widths.

  6. 6Stable search resultsDone

    Results stay on screen (dimmed) while a new search loads, with no placeholder swap and no size change. If the server is briefly busy, the app quietly retries instead of showing an error.

  7. 7Stable valuation resultsDone
